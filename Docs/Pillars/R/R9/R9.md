# R9 — Optimize connection quality and remove avoidable detours

**Status:** target behavior.

**Primary owner:** a dedicated `PhysicalDesign/Objectives/` package, with
candidate production under `PhysicalDesign/Routing/`.

## Goal

Evaluate physical-design quality using routed connection cost and congestion,
not only the placed footprint. Generate direct and low-bend alternatives and
replace avoidable winding routes even when the overall bounding box does not
shrink.

## Objective dimensions

- Total unique routed material.
- Individual source-to-sink path length.
- Shared-tree reuse without double-counting common wire.
- Bottleneck demand relative to capacity.
- Remaining pin-escape and interface capacity.
- Bends and vertical transitions.
- Supports and repeaters.
- Propagation delay and supported timing constraints.
- Combined placed-and-routed footprint, height, and maximum dimension.

Hard electrical or resource conflicts are validity failures, not large cost
penalties. Estimates, lower bounds, and measurements of completed candidates
remain explicitly classified.

## Route-shortening behavior

- Generate direct and low-bend routes using actual pin access.
- Search new legal wire positions rather than only removing wire from an
  existing route graph.
- Preserve every required sink, branch, ownership constraint, and directed
  behavior.
- Rebuild affected support, repeater, resource, and ownership data.
- Ask the shared redstone model to check the proposed replacement.
- Commit a replacement only when it is legal, globally compatible, and better
  under the current objective.
- Record why a shorter route was rejected, not explored, or unresolved.

## Stale-result rules

Geometry, feasibility, individual metric values, aggregate score, relative
ranking, and optimality are separate claims.

An old route may remain feasible while its congestion score or rank becomes
stale. Every affected metric is recomputed before comparison. An old optimality
claim survives only when the full candidate domain, objective version,
tie-breaking policy, and completeness evidence remain equivalent.

## Acceptance

- A legal shorter replacement inside an unchanged envelope beats the winding
  alternative.
- A visually shorter but physically illegal route is rejected with a concrete
  model reason.
- Shared fanout wire is not double-counted.
- Objective breakdowns are deterministic and report units and evidence class.
- Physical validation is unchanged or stronger after optimization.
