# R3 — Generate folded and fully oriented alternatives early

**Status:** target behavior.

**Primary owner:** `PhysicalDesign/Placement/`.

## Goal

Generate legal rotations, mirrors, folded layouts, serpentine layouts, mixed
directions, and alternative pin-facing arrangements during initial candidate
construction. Logical depth describes dependency order; it does not force a
physical axis or monotonically increasing coordinate.

Folding is an alternative, not an automatic winner. The combined placed and
routed result decides whether it is better.

## Required candidate dependencies

Every alternative declares:

- Normalized logical topology, signal roles, fanout, and endpoints.
- Cell, template, technology, and redstone-model versions.
- Exact cell transforms, rotations, and mirror states.
- Occupied-block claims.
- Support and required-air claims.
- Electrical-influence envelopes.
- Pin roles and pin-access alternatives.
- Required escape corridors.
- Boundary and channel-capacity assumptions.
- Relevant neighboring placement and interface assumptions.
- External reservation dependencies.
- Estimated metric dependencies and objective version.

## Selective stale-result rules

An older folded or oriented alternative is stale by default. It may be
salvaged only at the smallest scope proven under [N6](../../N/N6/N6.md).

Salvage requires all applicable conditions below:

1. The normalized logical subgraph, signal roles, fanout, reconvergence, and
   required endpoints remain compatible.
2. Every retained template, transform, orientation, mirror state, and port
   mapping remains legal under the current redstone model.
3. Occupancy, support, headroom, required air, powered-block influence, dust
   reach, repeater facing, and other declared influence claims remain
   compatible.
4. Every reused pin-access and escape claim remains valid against current
   neighboring claims, reservations, and capacities.
5. Relevant external changes are unchanged or proven non-interfering; being
   outside the cells' occupied coordinates is insufficient.
6. If only part of an orientation portfolio remains valid, only that part is
   retained.
7. Geometric feasibility and ranking are reviewed independently. A feasible
   placement may require new congestion, route-length, and incumbent-relative
   scores.
8. A no-good for one orientation or interface assignment does not reject a
   sibling orientation unless its proof explicitly covers it.
9. An optimality claim survives only when the complete candidate domain,
   constraints, objective, and competing alternatives remain equivalent.
10. Salvage makes an alternative eligible for current evaluation; it never
    commits the placement by itself.

The governing rule is:

> The cells did not move is never sufficient evidence. Ports, claims,
> influence, capacity assumptions, and the relevant neighborhood must still
> mean the same thing.

## Acceptance

- All supported rotations and mirrors have orientation-specific fixtures.
- A legal folded candidate can beat a linear candidate when its completed
  routed objective is better.
- An unchanged cell placement with newly blocked pin access is rejected.
- Partial orientation salvage retains only independently certified variants.
- Introducing a new alternative invalidates an old optimality claim without
  invalidating independently preserved feasibility.
