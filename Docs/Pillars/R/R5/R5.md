# R5 — Generalize by topology and reuse compatible work

**Status:** target behavior.

**Primary owner:** `PhysicalDesign/Routing/Regions/`.

## Goal

Solve and reuse physical-design work through normalized topology and explicit
interface contracts rather than circuit names, gate names, or fixture-specific
exceptions.

## Required behavior

- Normalize connectivity, ordered port roles, branch structure, reconvergence,
  and multiple-output relationships.
- Define explicit region and boundary contracts.
- Reuse compatible local domains, proofs, route trees, and geometry.
- Preserve valid work when another region changes.
- Include relevant cell, orientation, model, policy, topology, boundary, and
  external-reservation identities in reusable results.
- Keep reuse diagnostics explicit about assumptions and work avoided.
- Make HalfAdder and CLA4 work through the same general mechanisms used by the
  other circuits.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Circuit-name or gate-name branches.
- Cache identity based only on a fixture name or unordered signal set.
- Reusing one region's mutable solver session as another region's contract.
- Extending a local proof beyond its declared boundary or domain.

## Reuse and stale-result rules

Matching normalized topology is necessary but not sufficient. Ordered port
roles, orientations, model versions, policy/domain versions, boundary
contracts, and relevant external reservations must match or be certified as
non-interfering under [N6](../../N/N6/N6.md).

Changed dependencies invalidate affected work only. A broad snapshot mismatch
does not require discarding an independently certified region, but a shared
topology label does not excuse missing dependency proof.

## Acceptance

- General rules cover branching signals, reconvergence, dense interfaces, and
  multiple outputs.
- Equivalent normalized regions reuse completed work across distinct circuit
  instances.
- A changed external reservation invalidates the dependent region result.
- Unchanged sibling regions remain reusable after a scoped repair.
- No algorithmic branch depends on `HalfAdder`, `CLA4`, or another circuit name.
