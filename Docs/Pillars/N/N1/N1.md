# N1 — Explicit search outcomes and independent state axes

**Status:** necessary requirement; a bounded Runtime slice is implemented, but
full N1 acceptance remains unestablished.

**Primary owner:** `PhysicalDesign/Contracts/`.

## Goal

Prevent incomplete, stale, cancelled, or failed work from being mistaken for a
proof or accepted result. Search outcome, lifecycle, freshness, claim strength,
and commit eligibility are independent properties.

## Current frontier

The current Runtime owner candidate provides immutable, versioned work/results
with the five axes independent for one synchronous and one spawned unary path.
The spawned path now uses owned one-shot children: deadline termination, result
limit, and child `SystemExit` remain typed unresolved outcomes with no value or
proof. Post-start parent `KeyboardInterrupt` and `SystemExit` objects remain
present by identity in the owned receipt and propagate unchanged after successful
cleanup. If cleanup cannot acknowledge exit, reap, and release by its exact
cutoff, a dedicated typed exception carries every still-owned handle and its
latest receipt and preserves that exact parent control as its cause instead of
inventing a terminal result. The parent validates every decoded child result
against the admitted task, operation, scope, freshness, per-request cap,
aggregate grant, and outcome/value/proof relationship; a mismatch becomes an
original-request-bound `WorkerFailure` with no value or proof. Serial and
spawned symbolic adapters pre-admit each expansion, report authoritative
performed work no greater than the caller's shared cap, and publish factor,
port-pair, higher-order, and unary mutable cache/proof state only after the
complete relation validates. This remains a bounded Runtime owner candidate
pending independent review, not repository-wide N1 acceptance or a
Router-consumed checkpoint.

## Required axes

```text
Search outcome:
    Prepared | Infeasible | Unresolved

Lifecycle:
    Queued | Running | Yielding | Paused
    | CancellationRequested | TerminationRequested
    | Completed | TerminatedGracefully | TerminatedForced | Failed

Freshness:
    Current | StaleUnreviewed | Salvaged | SalvageRejected

Claim strength:
    Candidate | Feasible | Complete | Optimal
    | InfeasibilityProof | Continuation

Commit eligibility:
    Ineligible | RevalidationRequired | Eligible
```

These axes must not be collapsed into ambiguous booleans such as `Success`,
`Complete`, or `Valid` without the corresponding typed meaning.

## Outcome rules

- `Infeasible` requires a complete proof over an explicitly identified domain.
- `Unresolved` covers deadline, work-cap, cancellation, incomplete exploration,
  incomplete input, and worker failure without creating a negative proof.
- A legal candidate may remain usable while optimality is unknown.
- A proof applies only to its declared assumptions and scope.
- Demotion and termination do not establish a routing outcome.
- Freshness does not establish correctness or commitment.
- Salvage under [N6](../N6/N6.md) does not strengthen claim scope.

## Acceptance

- Every terminal operation returns one typed outcome.
- Timeout, cancellation, worker failure, incomplete exploration, and
  infeasibility remain distinguishable in serialization and diagnostics.
- No partial search produces a complete no-good or infeasibility claim.
- Existing callers cannot infer commit eligibility from result population.
